How Fort Worth Homeowners Can Extend HVAC Life

A little routine care makes all the difference between a system that runs for years and one that quits in the middle of the season. Our advice for homeowners is a short list: a new filter every one to three months, at least two feet of open space around the outdoor condenser, a spring rinse of the condenser coil, and two professional tune-ups a year, so small issues get caught early.

The age of your equipment matters just as much. With regular service, a central air conditioner usually runs 12 to 15 years, a furnace 15 to 20, and a heat pump 10 to 15. Once a system passes that range and starts showing warning signs, like rising energy bills, rooms that never reach the setpoint, or one repair after another, it's smart to start planning a replacement. Replacing on your own schedule is cheaper and is far less stressful than scrambling for a new system during the worst heat wave of summer.

Minor issues don't stay minor when they're ignored. Take a weak capacitor: replacing it is a short, inexpensive repair, but left alone for a season, it can take the compressor with it, and suddenly a minor fix becomes a full system replacement. That's why we encourage homeowners to save our number in their phone and call at the first sign of trouble. Catching it early saves money and prevents a miserable night.

Common HVAC Problems in Fort Worth

Most local HVAC failures announce themselves before they happen. A breaker that trips more than once, a condenser that hums louder each month, rooms drifting further from the thermostat setting, energy bills creeping up with nothing else changed. These are the early symptoms of failing capacitors, refrigerant leaks, dirty coils, and duct problems, and caught early they're a quick visit. Ignored, they take out compressors and heat exchangers, the two most expensive parts in the system. We diagnose the cause, not just the symptom, so small problems stay small.
